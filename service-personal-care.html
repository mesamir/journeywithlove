Personal Care

Respectful, hands-on support with the personal routines that keep someone comfortable, clean and feeling like themselves.

Care Service

Personal care, delivered with dignity

Personal care is the most intimate part of what we do. It's the help someone needs to wash, dress, groom and feel ready for the day — and it's the moment when dignity matters most.

At Willowbrook, personal care is delivered by a small, consistent team who know each resident's preferences, routines and boundaries. We don't rush. We don't decide for people. We ask, we listen, and we help in the way the resident wants to be helped.

  • Privacy maintained during personal care
  • Consistent caregivers who know the routine
  • Preferences for timing, products and rituals honoured
  • Honest communication if abilities change

What to Expect

How it works in practice

  • A written personal care plan agreed with the resident and family before any care begins
  • Caregivers trained on the specific routines, preferences and boundaries in that plan
  • Privacy respected at every step — doors, curtains, clothing, and who is present
  • Care delivered at the resident's pace, not on a caregiver's schedule
  • Regular review — abilities and preferences change, and the plan changes with them
  • Honest escalation if a need falls outside the home's licensed scope of care

Who this service is for

Personal care is for residents who need hands-on help with some of the routines most people do independently — whether because of age, illness, injury, or a progressive condition. It's also for families who want to stop worrying about whether their loved one is getting the help they need.

Whether we can meet a specific person's needs depends on an individual assessment and the home's licensed scope of care. Please ask before assuming.
