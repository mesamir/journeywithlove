Medication Assistance

Timely reminders and documented support with medications — in line with the home's licensed scope of care and each resident's prescribing instructions.

Care Service

Medication support without guesswork

Missed doses, doubled doses and confusion about which pill goes with which meal are some of the most common concerns families raise. In a small home, where the same team supports the same residents every day, those risks are far easier to manage.

We support residents with medications according to their prescribing instructions and the home's licensed scope of care. We keep records, watch for changes, and escalate promptly when something seems wrong.

  • Timely reminders at the correct times
  • Records kept as required by regulation
  • Coordination with prescribing professionals
  • Prompt escalation when something seems wrong

What's Included

The support we can provide

Reminders & prompts

Timely reminders at the times agreed in the care plan and prescribing instructions.

Documentation

Records kept of what was given, when, and by whom — as required by regulation.

Refill awareness

Noticing when supply is running low and raising it before it becomes a problem.

Professional coordination

Communication with prescribing doctors and pharmacies, with the resident's consent.

Observation

Watching for side effects, changes in condition, or anything that warrants a call to the doctor.

Escalation

Prompt action if a dose is missed, doubled, or the resident shows signs of a reaction.

The exact medication support the home can legally provide depends on its licensed scope of care. Not all homes are licensed to do everything listed here. Confirm the specifics with the owner before assuming.

What to Expect

How it works in practice

  • A written medication support plan agreed with the resident, family and (where relevant) prescribing doctor
  • Caregivers trained on that plan and refreshed as medications change
  • Records available to families on request
  • Clear procedures for missed doses and adverse reactions
  • Honest communication if a resident's medication needs exceed the home's licence

Who this service is for

Medication assistance is for residents who take regular prescriptions and benefit from reminders, documentation, or coordination between multiple prescribers. It's not a substitute for nursing care — if a resident needs clinical medication management beyond the home's licence, we'll say so and help explore alternatives.
