Amenities & Living Environment

A house designed to be comfortable, accessible and safe — not a clinical setting.

Images and amenity descriptions on this page are illustrative example content. Verified details of the actual property will replace them before launch.

Rooms & Spaces

Where residents spend their days

Private & Shared Bedrooms

Furnished rooms with space for personal belongings, a comfortable bed and good natural light. Residents are encouraged to make their room their own.

Comfortable Living Room

A shared space for conversation, television, reading and family visits. Soft seating, good lighting and space for everyone.

Dining Room

A table where everyone eats together — meals are a social occasion, not a delivery. Family are welcome to join when they visit.

Home Kitchen

Meals prepared on site, with residents able to participate in cooking when they wish. The smell of something on the stove is part of what makes it home.

Accessible Bathrooms

Grab bars and accessible fixtures to support safe, independent use where possible. Privacy is always respected.

Outdoor Garden

Seating and raised beds for fresh air, gentle activity and time outside. A safe, enclosed area for short walks.

Activity Spaces

Tables for puzzles, cards and crafts, plus room for music and conversation. Activities are chosen around real interests.

Quiet Corners

Spaces to sit quietly, read or rest away from the bustle of shared rooms. Not everyone wants company all the time — and that's fine.

Outdoor Seating

Shaded seating for warmer days and a safe, enclosed area for short walks. Fresh air is good for everyone.

Safety & Accessibility

Features that support confident living

These features are typical of an Adult Family Home and are listed here as example content. The exact features of this property will be confirmed and documented before launch.

Bathroom safety

Grab bars, non-slip surfaces, and fixtures that support independent use where possible.

Clear pathways

Well-lit, unobstructed paths through the home and reduced trip hazards.

Fire & CO safety

Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, with regular checks.

Secure entry

Controlled entry and exit points, so residents are protected without feeling confined.

Mobility accommodation

Space and access for mobility equipment where assessed and appropriate.

Emergency preparedness

Clear procedures and accessible ways to call for help when needed.
