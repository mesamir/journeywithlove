Why Work Here

Caregiving that actually feels like care

In a large facility, caregivers often see dozens of residents a day, with barely enough time to know any of them. In a small home, the opposite is true: a handful of residents, a consistent team, and the time to actually do the work properly.

If you became a caregiver because you wanted to make a real difference in someone's daily life — not just tick boxes on a chart — this might be the place for you.

  • Know every resident as a person, not a room number
  • Consistent schedule and a small, stable team
  • Real input into how care is delivered
  • A home environment, not an institutional one

What We Look For

The qualities that matter here

  • Genuine interest in older people as individuals
  • Patience — real patience, not performative patience
  • Reliability: showing up, on time, consistently
  • Willingness to do the physical work of caregiving
  • Clear communication with residents, families and colleagues
  • Respect for confidentiality and dignity
