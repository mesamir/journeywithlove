Care Philosophy

How we hire, and why

We hire for temperament as much as for training. Patience, consistency and genuine interest in older people matter more than a long list of certificates — and the residents notice the difference. Some specific things we look for:

  • Willingness to slow down and do things at the resident's pace
  • Comfort with the physical work of caregiving
  • Ability to notice small changes and report them
  • Genuine curiosity about residents as people
  • Reliability — showing up, on time, consistently
  • Respect for family involvement and communication

Staff commitment

Our team commits to confidentiality, to respectful personal care, and to reporting anything that concerns them — to the administrator and, where relevant, to families.
