Memory Support

Calm routines, gentle orientation cues and patient companionship for residents living with memory change.

Care Service

Meeting the person, not the diagnosis

Memory change doesn't erase who someone is. It changes how they experience the world, and how the world needs to meet them. In a small home, where routines stay familiar and faces stay consistent, that meeting is far easier.

Our memory support is offered only where it falls within the home's licensed scope of care and after an individual assessment. We are honest about what we can and cannot safely provide.

  • Predictable daily routines that reduce confusion
  • Patient, unhurried communication
  • A safe, familiar physical environment
  • Activities chosen for the person, not the diagnosis

What's Included

The support we can provide

Predictable routines

Meals, activities and rest at consistent times, so the day feels familiar even when memory doesn't.

Gentle orientation

Calm reminders of time, place and people — offered lightly, never argued about.

Companionship

Patient company, conversation and shared activities at a comfortable pace.

Meaningful activity

Music, simple crafts, folding laundry, looking at photos — activities that work with remaining abilities.

Safety supervision

Watching for wandering, confusion, or anything that risks safety — without surveillance.

Mealtime support

Prompts, patience and dietary adaptation so that meals remain nourishing and social.

Memory support is offered only where it falls within the home's licensed scope of care and after an individual assessment. Not all homes are licensed for this. Please ask directly.

What to Expect

How it works in practice

  • A care plan built around the resident's history, preferences and remaining abilities
  • Consistency in caregivers, routines and physical environment
  • Regular review as the resident's needs change
  • Honest conversations with families about what's ahead — and when a different setting may be needed
  • Clear escalation if a need exceeds the home's licensed scope of care

Who this service is for

Memory support is for residents living with mild to moderate memory change who can still be safely supported in a residential setting. It is not a secure dementia unit. If a resident's needs require a secured memory care environment, we will say so honestly and help the family explore alternatives.
