Costs & Payment

Understanding what affects cost, and the questions to ask before signing anything.

No prices are listed on this page. Rates vary by home, location and level of care, and must be confirmed directly. This page explains the general structure — not our specific pricing.

What Affects Cost

The factors that shape a monthly rate

Room type

Private vs shared bedrooms, and whether a bathroom is en-suite or shared.

Level of care

More hands-on support — for example with mobility, memory or continence — typically means a higher monthly rate.

Location

Rates vary considerably between neighborhoods and between homes within the same city.

Meals & services

Whether meals, laundry, housekeeping and activities are included or itemised separately.

One-time fees

Some homes charge a move-in or community fee; others don't. Always ask.

External services

Physiotherapy, hospice, specialist nursing or transport may be billed separately by outside providers.

Paying for Care

How families typically cover the cost

Private funds

Savings, pensions, Social Security and — in some cases — the proceeds of selling a home. This is the most common source of payment for Adult Family Home care.

Long-term care insurance

Some policies cover in-home and residential care. Review the policy carefully with an advisor, since coverage terms vary widely.

State-funded programs

Some Adult Family Homes accept state-funded programs for eligible residents. Availability varies by home, by program and by level of need. Ask directly.

Veterans' benefits

Veterans and surviving spouses may be eligible for programs such as Aid & Attendance. Speak with a veterans' benefits advisor about eligibility.

This page does not provide financial or legal advice. Eligibility for state and federal programs depends on individual circumstances. Speak with a qualified benefits advisor.

Before You Sign

Questions to ask any Adult Family Home

  • What is the monthly base rate, and what does it include?
  • What level-of-care upgrades exist, and what triggers them?
  • Are there any one-time fees (move-in, community, deposit)?
  • What is not included (personal supplies, transport, outside services)?
  • How much notice is required if the resident moves out?
  • How are rate increases communicated, and how often do they occur?
  • Which payment sources does the home accept?
  • What happens if funds run out — what's the policy?
