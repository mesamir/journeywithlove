Welcome to Willowbrook

Residential senior care that feels like family

An Adult Family Home is a licensed residential setting where a small number of adults live together and receive care in a real house — not an institution. That smaller scale changes everything: familiar faces every day, meals at a shared table, and care built around one person rather than a schedule.

At Willowbrook, we keep our resident count intentionally low so that each person is genuinely known — their routines, their preferences, their stories, and the things that make them feel like themselves. We believe dignity is not a program you add to a care plan. It's the way you treat someone every single day.

  • One consistent caregiving team, not rotating strangers
  • Care plans reviewed with the family, not decided above them
  • Real home, real meals, real community
  • Family involvement welcomed at every stage

Why families choose an Adult Family Home

Assisted living serves many people well. But for some families, a smaller, more personal setting is exactly what they've been looking for.

Comparison of Adult Family Home and larger assisted living facility
Consideration Adult Family Home Assisted Living Facility

Our Care

Support with the things that matter every day

Every service is delivered by our own caregivers and tailored through an individual care plan agreed with the resident and their family.

Personalized Care

A care plan built around one resident's needs, preferences and daily rhythm — reviewed regularly and adjusted as things change.

Assistance with Daily Living

Respectful help with bathing, grooming, dressing, toileting and mobility — always at the resident's pace and with their dignity intact.

Medication Assistance

Reminders and support with medications in line with the home's licensed scope of care and the resident's prescribing instructions.

Memory Support

Calm routines, gentle orientation cues and patient companionship for residents living with memory change. Offered subject to assessment.

Companionship

Conversation, shared meals, games, music and time outdoors — because emotional wellbeing matters as much as physical care.

Nutritious Meals

Home-cooked meals and snacks planned around dietary needs, cultural preferences and the simple pleasure of eating together.

Mobility Assistance

Safe transfer and walking support, plus help staying as active and independent as is safely possible.

Supervision & Safety

Awake or on-call staffing arrangements, safety checks and a home adapted for accessible, confident living.

Housekeeping & Laundry

Cleaning, linen changes and laundry handled for residents, so the home stays comfortable and families can simply visit.
