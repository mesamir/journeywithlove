Draft — requires review. This policy has not been reviewed against Washington State privacy law or federal requirements. It must not be published until a qualified professional has reviewed and approved it. It makes no claim of HIPAA compliance.

1. Information we collect

This website collects information you voluntarily enter into its forms: your name, email address, phone number, relationship to a prospective resident, preferred contact method, general care requirements, preferred dates and any message you write.

You are asked not to submit medical records, diagnoses, payment details or other sensitive information through this website.

2. How information is used

  • To respond to enquiries and tour requests
  • To discuss care needs at a general level
  • To improve the usefulness of the website

Information is not sold, and is not used for unrelated marketing.

3. Contact forms

In this prototype, forms do not transmit data to a server and do not send email. Submissions are stored only in your own browser's local storage and can be cleared at any time. In a production version, form submissions would be transmitted to the organisation's email or case management system.

4. Data storage

Prototype data is stored in your browser under the keys afh_contact_messages, afh_admission_inquiries, afh_tour_requests and afh_cookie_preferences. It is accessible to anyone with access to this browser profile.

5. Cookies and local storage

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies in this prototype. It uses browser local storage to retain demo form submissions.

6. Third-party services

The prototype loads Google Fonts and Font Awesome from third-party content delivery networks. Those providers may receive your IP address and user agent as part of normal HTTP requests. No analytics or advertising services are integrated in this prototype.

7. Data security

No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. In production, reasonable technical and organisational measures would be applied to protect personal information. This prototype should not be used to collect real personal information.

8. Data retention

Prototype data remains in your browser until you clear it. A production policy would define specific retention periods for enquiries, applications and resident records.

9. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information held about you. To exercise these rights in a production system, contact the organisation using the details below.
