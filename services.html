Our Care Services

Every service below is delivered by our own caregivers and shaped through an individual care plan agreed with the resident and their family.

Overview

What we provide, and how

Care at Willowbrook isn't a menu you pick from once. It's a relationship that evolves as needs change — always with the resident's choices and the family's input at the centre.

Individual care plans

Each resident has a written plan, updated as needs change and reviewed with the family.

Licensed scope of care

We only accept residents whose needs we can genuinely and safely meet within our license.

Ongoing reassessment

Needs change. We reassess regularly and talk about adjustments before they become urgent.

Personalized Care

Hands-on support with the personal routines that keep someone comfortable and well-groomed, delivered at the resident's own pace and with their preferences respected.

What residents can expect

  • Help chosen by the resident, not imposed on them
  • Consistent caregivers who know their routine
  • Privacy maintained during personal care
  • Preferences for timing, products and routines honoured

Benefits for families

  • Confidence that daily needs are covered
  • Open reporting when something changes
  • A familiar team you can speak to directly
Learn more about personal care

Activities of Daily Living (ADLs)

Support with the everyday tasks that make independent living possible — bathing and grooming, dressing, toileting, eating and moving safely around the home.

What residents can expect

  • Help with bathing, grooming and dressing
  • Assistance with toileting and continence care
  • Encouragement to do what they can independently
  • Support that adapts as abilities change

Benefits for families

  • Reduced risk of falls and hygiene-related illness
  • A clear picture of changing care needs
  • Less worry about what happens between visits
Learn more about personal care

Mobility & Transfer Assistance

Safe support for walking, standing and moving between bed, chair and bathroom, using appropriate techniques and equipment where required.

What residents can expect

  • Assistance tailored to their current ability
  • Encouragement to stay active where safe
  • Prompt help when they need it
  • Proper equipment used where required

Benefits for families

  • Lower risk of injury from unsafe transfers
  • Continued independence within safe limits
  • Fewer trips to the emergency room
Learn more about mobility and transfer assistance

Medication Assistance

Reminders and support with medications in line with the home's licensed scope of care and the resident's prescribing instructions.

What residents can expect

  • Timely reminders at the correct times
  • Records kept as required by regulation
  • Prompt escalation if something seems wrong
  • Coordination with prescribing professionals

Benefits for families

  • Fewer missed doses
  • Confidence that changes are noticed
  • A single point of contact for medication questions
Learn more about medication assistance

Memory Support

Calm routines, gentle orientation cues and patient companionship for residents living with memory change. Offered only where it falls within the home's licensed scope of care and after assessment.

What residents can expect

  • Predictable daily routines that reduce confusion
  • Patient, unhurried communication
  • A safe, familiar physical environment
  • Activities chosen for the person, not the diagnosis

Benefits for families

  • Honest guidance on whether the home is the right fit
  • Regular updates as needs change
  • Realistic conversations about what's ahead
Learn more about memory support

Companionship & Social Activities

Conversation, shared meals, games, music, crafts and time outdoors. Emotional wellbeing is not a bonus to physical care — it's part of it.

What residents can expect

  • Company at meals and in common spaces
  • Activities matched to real interests
  • Encouragement, never pressure, to participate
  • Quiet time available when they want it

Benefits for families

  • Less isolation and loneliness
  • A resident who feels like part of a community
  • Something to talk about during visits
Learn more about companionship and social activities

Meals, Nutrition & Hydration

Home-cooked meals and snacks planned around dietary needs and personal preference, served at a shared table where conversation is part of the meal.

What residents can expect

  • Meals adapted to dietary and cultural preferences
  • Snacks available between meals
  • Company at the table, not meals eaten alone
  • Input into the menu on a regular basis

Benefits for families

  • Confidence that nutrition is being managed
  • Fewer worries about isolation and loneliness
  • One less thing to coordinate
Learn more about meals, nutrition and hydration

Housekeeping & Laundry

Cleaning, linen changes and personal laundry handled for residents, so the home stays comfortable and families can simply visit rather than work.

What residents can expect

  • Clean rooms and shared spaces
  • Laundry returned promptly
  • Belongings treated with care
  • Personal items arranged their way

Benefits for families

  • Visit time spent together, not on chores
  • One less thing to coordinate
  • Confidence that personal items are safe
Learn more about housekeeping and laundry

Supervision & Safety

Around-the-clock supervision, safety checks and a home adapted for accessible, confident living — with the level of oversight matched to each resident's needs.

What residents can expect

  • Staff available at all times
  • A home adapted for safe movement
  • Regular safety checks
  • Clear procedures for emergencies

Benefits for families

  • Peace of mind overnight and at weekends
  • Reduced worry about emergencies
  • Confidence that someone is always there
Learn more about supervision and safety

Important note about services

Every service listed here is subject to an individual care assessment and to the home's actual licensed scope of care. Not every service is appropriate for every resident, and some may be arranged through outside providers.

This page does not claim that the home is licensed for skilled nursing, hospice, or any specialty care not explicitly confirmed by the owner. Please discuss your loved one's specific needs with us before assuming a service is available.
